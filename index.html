curl -sL trystk.dev/i | sh
Then use /find-solution in Claude Code

What it does

  1. 1. Scans your stack — reads package.json, docker-compose, .env to understand what you already use
  2. 2. Asks the right questions — volume, MVP vs production, hard constraints
  3. 3. Decomposes the task — splits it into sub-problems and weighs self-host / open-source against managed SaaS, not just one category
  4. 4. Searches and cross-checks — cross-checks vendor pricing against practitioner reports (GitHub issues, Hacker News), not just marketing
  5. 5. Compares and scores — reliability / anti-ban, maintenance burden, feature match, integration effort, and pricing at your scale
  6. 6. Recommends with evidence — every claim links to a source

Why not just prompt it yourself?

A plain prompt gives you a generic list. This skill:

  • Knows your actual stack (scans project files, not guessing)
  • Decomposes the task and weighs self-host vs SaaS (a $2/mo build can beat a $47/mo service)
  • Cross-checks vendor pricing against practitioner reports — GitHub, HN — not just marketing
  • Scores objectively (you see the breakdown, not just "I think X is good")
