How it works

1

Scan

Reads your package.json, docker-compose, .env to understand your stack and existing integrations.

2

Search

Searches the web for matching services, fetches real pricing pages and documentation.

3

Compare

Scores each option on SDK fit, free tier, features, DX, and pricing. Gives a clear recommendation.

Why not just prompt it yourself?

A plain prompt gives generic answers. This skill is different.

Knows your stack

Scans your project files — package.json, docker-compose, .env — and recommends services with a native SDK for your language and framework.

Real pricing, not guesses

Fetches actual pricing pages and docs at runtime. No hallucinated numbers from 2024 training data.

Objective scoring

Every recommendation is scored on SDK fit, free tier, feature match, DX, and cost. You see the breakdown, not just "I think X is good."
